# 1. Product

## The problem

| Who | Pain |
| --- | --- |
| **Travellers** | Spend hours on blogs and maps. Miss the best local things because they're "between" cities. Often pay a higher "tourist price". |
| **Local hosts** | Cooks, farmers, craftspeople, guides in small towns. Great skills, no website, no way to reach tourists who drive past. |
| **The country** | Tourist money stays in a few hot spots. Spend per visitor is falling. |

## Our answer

A web app (installable on the phone, works offline) where a traveller:

1. Enters **From** and **To** (for example Colombo → Ella).
2. Sees experiences **along the road**, in the order they'll pass them: *"km 42 · Avissawella · you pass ~09:10"*.
3. Adds the ones they like to a **trip**.
4. Books with a **15% deposit**.
5. Gets a **ticket that works with no signal**.
6. Meets the host, pays the rest in cash, leaves a review.

## Who it's for

| User | What they want | Device |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Independent traveller** (main user) | Hire a car/driver, tuk-tuk or train. Wants real, local, fair-priced things to do on the way. | Laptop before the trip, phone during it |
| **Host** | More guests, fair pay, no tech headache | Phone (often basic), or our team does it for them |
| **Our team** (staff / admin) | Add hosts, check quality, handle problems | Staff portal on a laptop |

| Moment | Where | Must work |
| --- | --- | --- |
