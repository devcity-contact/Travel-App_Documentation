# 4. Business model

## How we make money

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Deposit** | Traveller pays **15%** of the price online when booking. This is our commission. |
| **Balance** | Traveller pays **85%** in cash straight to the host on the day. |
| **Host cost** | Free to join. Hosts pay nothing upfront. |
| **Money we hold** | None. Hosts get their money directly. Low risk, no payout system needed at launch. |

## Example

| | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Cooking class, $40 × 2 people | $80 |
| Paid online now (15%) | **$12** → our income |
| Paid to the host on the day | **$68** |

## Price rules

- One fixed price for everyone. No foreigner markup.
- The host can't charge more on the day than the price shown.
- Travellers can report overcharging. Hosts get a warning or are removed.
- Hosts can't offer a cheaper price off the platform (host agreement).

## Why hosts like it

| For the host | |
| --- | --- |
| No upfront cost | Pay only when you get a booking |
| Cash on the day | 85% in your hand, no waiting for payouts |
| No tech needed | Our team can list you and manage your page |
| Fewer no-shows | Guests already paid a deposit |
