# 8. Partnerships

We're looking for partners who help us launch faster and reach more travellers: cloud and technology programs, tourism bodies, and early investors.

## Our pitch in 30 seconds

> Every day, millions of tourists drive past real local experiences they never knew existed — a village cooking class, a tea estate walk, a mask carver's workshop. Booking sites only show city tours, and maps don't sell anything.
>
> **We show travellers real local experiences along the road they're already travelling, in the order they'll pass them.** They book in a minute with a 15% deposit, get a ticket that works offline, and pay the host the rest in cash.
>
> We start in Sri Lanka — 2.36 million visitors in 2025, a record — and every new country is just data, not new code.

## In one paragraph

> Our platform, built by Devcity, is a route-based experience marketplace. Travellers enter where they're going, and we show verified local experiences along the road — cooking, crafts, farming, nature, culture — in road order with detour times. They book with a 15% online deposit (our revenue) and pay the host the rest on the day, so hosts get cash immediately and we never hold their money. Our team lists hosts who aren't online, verifies each one in person, and enforces one fair price for everyone. The app works on weak signal with offline tickets. We launch in Sri Lanka and expand country by country as data.
