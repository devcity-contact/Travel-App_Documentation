# 5. Market

## Sri Lanka at a glance (2025)

| Number | Value | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tourist arrivals | **2,362,521** (record, +15% vs 2024) | SLTDA Year in Review 2025 |
| Tourism earnings | **~US$ 3.2 billion** (+1.6% only) | Central Bank of Sri Lanka |
| Earnings per visitor | ~US$ 1,363 (down from ~US$ 1,543 in 2024) | CBSL / press |
| Daily spend per visitor | ~US$ 148 (revised from US$ 171) | SLTDA survey |
| National target | US$ 5 billion a year | Government |

**What this tells us:** more people are coming, but each spends less. The country needs ways to get visitors to spend on real local things, outside the hot spots. That's us.

## Why Sri Lanka is a great first market

- Small island, **road trips are the normal way to travel** (car + driver, tuk-tuk, the famous train).
- Main tourist roads are few and well known: Colombo–Galle, Colombo–Kandy, Kandy–Ella, Kandy–Sigiriya, Galle–Ella.
- Thousands of village hosts with real skills and **no online presence**.
- "Tourist price" is a common complaint. One fair price is a strong promise.
- Weak mobile signal in hill country and rural areas → offline matters.

## Size of the opportunity (simple)

> Example math, not a forecast.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Visitors per year | 2.36 million |
| If **2%** book one experience through us | ~47,000 bookings |
| At ~$7.50 income per booking | **~$350,000 a year**, Sri Lanka only |

Growth comes from: more bookings per traveller, more routes, and **new countries added as data** (same code).

## Next markets (same model fits)

Countries where travellers drive between towns and local hosts are offline: for example Vietnam, Indonesia, Thailand, India (Kerala, Rajasthan), Nepal, Morocco, Portugal. To be chosen later.

## Competitors

| Who | What they do well | Gap we fill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Viator, GetYourGuide, Klook | Big catalogues, trusted checkout | City tours by companies; nothing "on your road"; often tourist pricing |
| Airbnb Experiences | Beautiful listings, trust | Few in Sri Lanka; city-based; no route view |
| Google Maps | Roads and places | Doesn't sell or book local experiences |
| Wanderlog, Roadtrippers | Trip planning | Places only, no local hosts, no booking, weak outside US/EU |
| Local tour agents / drivers | Local knowledge | Fixed packages, commission-driven, not transparent |

**Our one-line edge:** the only app that links **road → local host → booking → offline ticket**.
